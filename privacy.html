Lotl privacy policy

Last updated 7 November 2026. Applies to the Lotl iOS and Android apps.

Lotl is a quit-vaping app with a pet. The short version: everything you type into Lotl stays on your phone. We run no accounts and no server of our own, and we never sell data.

Who is responsible

Lotl is published by Adam Zeraiki, an independent developer based in France, who is the data controller for the purposes of the GDPR. Contact: support@getlotl.app.

What stays on your phone

The following is stored only in the app's private storage on your device and is never transmitted to us:

  • Your pet's name and look, and the items you have unlocked.
  • Your answers to the setup quiz: device type, puffs per day, nicotine strength, weekly spend, triggers, reasons and past attempts.
  • Every puff you log, with its time, and every craving you ride out.
  • Your plan (quit date, daily limits) and your reminder settings.

Deleting the app deletes all of it. You can also wipe it from inside the app under Profile, "Start over".

What leaves your phone

ServiceWhat it receivesWhy
Apple App Store or Google Play Your purchase, handled entirely by Apple or Google under their own terms. We never see your payment details. To sell and renew Lotl Plus.
RevenueCat A random app-generated identifier, the purchase receipt, your device model and OS version, and your country as reported by the store. If you typed a friend's or creator's code during setup and you subscribe, that code is attached to the same random identifier so we can count how many subscriptions a recommendation brought. No name, email or health data. Policy: revenuecat.com/privacy. To check whether your Lotl Plus subscription is active and to restore it on a new phone.
PostHog (usage analytics) Anonymous events such as "finished onboarding", "opened paywall", "started craving ride", with the same random identifier, your app version, device model, OS version and country. We do not send your puff counts, your plan, your quiz answers or your pet's name. If you type a friend's or creator's code during setup, that code is sent with the "finished onboarding" and "started trial" events so we know which recommendation brought you. Policy: posthog.com/privacy. To see where people get stuck and improve the app.

No advertising networks, no social trackers, no data brokers.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. We do not run a push server and cannot send you messages. You can turn reminders off in the app or in your phone's settings at any time.

Health information

Lotl asks about your nicotine use so it can build your plan. That information is sensitive, which is exactly why it never leaves your phone. The recovery milestones shown in the app are general information from public health sources (each one links to its source inside the app) and are not medical advice. If you want help quitting, the app lists free quit lines; in France, Tabac info service is on 39 89.

Age

Lotl is for adults. It is rated 18+ on the App Store and is not directed at children.

Your rights

Because your data is on your device and not in our hands, access, correction and deletion are things you do yourself: open the app, or delete it. For the small amount of data held by RevenueCat or PostHog, write to support@getlotl.app with the random identifier shown under Profile, "About", and we will have it deleted. You can also lodge a complaint with the CNIL (cnil.fr) or your local data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes and the app shows a notice on next launch.
