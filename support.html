Lotl support

Answers to the questions we get most. If yours isn't here, email support@getlotl.app and you'll hear back within two working days.

Subscriptions

I bought Lotl Plus on another phone. How do I get it back?

Open Lotl, go to Profile and tap "Restore purchases". Make sure the phone is signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account that made the purchase. If it still doesn't show, email us the random identifier under Profile, "About".

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google, not by us. iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Lotl Plus, Cancel. Android: Play Store, your profile, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions, Lotl, Cancel. Cancelling during the free trial means you are never charged. You keep Plus until the end of the period you paid for.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are issued by Apple or Google. iPhone: reportaproblem.apple.com. Android: request it from your Play Store order history. We cannot issue refunds directly because we never hold your payment.

What is in Plus, exactly?

Adaptive re-planning when you slip, trigger and peak-hour insights, smart reminders timed to your usual vaping hours, and every cosmetic in the shop. Logging, the plan, craving rides and the basic shop are free and stay free.

Using the app

I logged a puff by mistake.

Tap "Undo last puff" under the ring on the home screen. For older days, go to Progress, "Edit days", and set the right number.

I went way over my limit. Is my plan ruined?

No. Lotl counts today's slip and carries on with tomorrow's limit. If you want the plan to re-adjust from today, open Profile, "Adjust plan". Your history is never erased.

Reminders don't arrive.

Check that notifications are allowed for Lotl in your phone's settings, that Focus or Do Not Disturb isn't silencing them, and that reminders are switched on under Profile, "Reminders". Reminders are scheduled on the phone itself, so they also stop if the app has been force-closed on some Android phones with aggressive battery saving.

Why does my lotl look sick?

The pet follows the pond, and the pond follows your logging. Several days over limit in a row make the water murky and the lotl pale. One good day starts clearing it, and a craving ridden out clears it faster.

How is "money saved" calculated?

From the weekly spend you entered at setup, spread over each day and compared with what you actually logged. "Nicotine avoided" uses an average of 0.0035 ml of liquid per puff, which is an estimate; the app says so next to the number.

Your data

Where is my data stored?

On your phone only. There is no account and no Lotl server. See the privacy policy for the two services that receive anonymous data (RevenueCat for purchases, PostHog for usage events).

How do I delete everything?

Profile, "Start over" wipes all data inside the app. Deleting the app does the same. Email us if you also want the anonymous records at RevenueCat or PostHog removed.

Can I move my data to a new phone?

Not yet. Your Plus subscription restores on the new phone, but logs stay on the old one. Sync is on the roadmap.

Need help quitting?

Lotl is a tool, not a doctor. Free, confidential help: in France, Tabac info service on 39 89 (tabac-info-service.fr); in the US, text DITCHVAPE to 88709 (This is Quitting, Truth Initiative); in the UK, NHS Better Health.
