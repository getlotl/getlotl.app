A day with Lotl

  • Log every puff

    One tap per puff, or +3 for a longer session. Made a mistake? Undo is right there.

  • Follow a plan that tapers

    Your daily limit steps down gently to your quit date. A slip is logged, not punished, and your history stays.

  • Ride out a craving

    Five minutes of breathing with your axolotl when the urge hits. Cravings usually last 5 to 10 minutes.

Craving length: U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, Every Try Counts.

Nobody dies in this pond.

Have a bad few days and the pond turns to swamp, then to a dead pond. Your axolotl never dies. Ride out one craving there and it starts breathing again.

Go quiet for a week and it hides under the lily pad until you come back. Nothing you earned is lost.
